BIOGRAPHY

Suzanne Collins began her professional writing career in 1991 working on several children's television shows. Her work included popular Nickelodeon productions such as Clarissa Explains It All and The Mystery Files of Shelby Woo, along with preschool shows like Little Bear and Oswald.

Learn More

She also co-wrote the animated Christmas special Santa, Baby! with Peter Bakalian and worked as Head Writer for Scholastic Entertainment's Clifford's Puppy Days.

While working on Generation O!, she met children's author and illustrator James Proimos, who encouraged her to explore writing children's books. This marked the beginning of her transition from television to literature.

The Beginning of The Underland Chronicles

Inspired by Alice in Wonderland, Collins began imagining what a fantasy adventure would look like for children growing up in an urban environment. Rather than falling down a rabbit hole, her character would enter an underground world through a manhole in New York City. This idea became Gregor the Overlander, the first book in her five-part fantasy series, The Underland Chronicles.

The Underland Chronicles follows Gregor as he discovers a hidden world beneath New York City. The series explores courage, friendship, and the consequences of war.

The Hunger Games: A Global Phenomenon

Published in September 2008, The Hunger Games introduced readers to a dystopian world shaped by war, survival, and political conflict. The series includes the original trilogy, The Hunger Games, Catching Fire, and Mockingjay, along with two prequels: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes and Sunrise on the Reaping. The books became an international success, appearing on major bestseller lists, including The New York Times, USA Today, and Publishers Weekly.

In 2024, the first illustrated edition of The Hunger Games, featuring artwork by Nico Delort, was published.

The series has also found success in audiobook format, with performances by Tatiana Maslany, Santino Fontana, and Jefferson White.

The Hunger Games on Stage

In October 2025, The Hunger Games expanded beyond books and films with its first live stage adaptation. The production began performances at the Troubadour Canary Wharf Theater in London.

Adapted by award-winning playwright Conor McPherson and directed by Matthew Dunster, the show brought the story to a new audience through live theater.

Beyond The Hunger Games

Alongside her fantasy and dystopian novels, Collins has written picture books exploring childhood experiences and family relationships.

When Charlie McButton Lost Power (2005) follows a boy obsessed with computer games who must deal with losing electricity.

Year of the Jungle (2013) is an autobiographical picture book inspired by the year Collins was six and her father was deployed to Vietnam.

Awards & Recognition

Throughout her career, Suzanne Collins has received recognition for her contributions to children's and young adult literature.

  • 2010: Named to the TIME 100 list and Entertainment Weekly's Entertainers of the Year list.
  • 2016: Received the Authors Guild Award for Distinguished Service to the Literary Community.
  • 2026: Nominated for the Astrid Lindgren Memorial Award.
